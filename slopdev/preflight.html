What this does. Runs the full setup path — WebKit primitive, module bases, gadget + syscall stub resolution, the rop-worker spawn and the libkernel call probe — then stops before the leak. About two minutes. It does not jailbreak and does not touch the kernel.

Why. A wrong offset shows up here in minutes instead of at minute 70 of a full run.

What a pass looks like. On-screen log ends with preflight complete - stopping here (?skipleak=1) and a “p2jb preflight done” notification. Look for the PREFLIGHT lines: A-POST returned … (stub path OK) means the syscall-stub call path works, and the canary thread spawn means the pthread path works.

What a fail looks like. no libkernel base - abort (base derivation is wrong), A-SKIP no getpid stub in the map (syscall map wrong), PREFLIGHT threw: …, or the page dying with no PREFLIGHT lines at all. Report the last line either way.
